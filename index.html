3 Our standards

Safety first. Always.

🚗

Drive safe. Leave early so you can drive like a grandma. Running late? Just let us know. Please don't make it a habit.

🏋️

Lift with your legs, not your back. Crates, pens, and feed bags add up.

🐜

Watch your step on the farm. Roots, fire ants and who knows what else. Look alive!

7 How we talk about us: FAQs & objections

Short, sweet lines you can say out loud. Tap a question.

👋Who are you?

We started as an educational company. People like STEM, but they LOVE goats and bunnies, so we bring both. We're Critters on Call, from Sheehan Homestead in Callahan.

🐴Why no horses or cows yet?

We love them and we're thinking about it! Bigger animals mean a bigger feed bill, more vet visits, a trailer and more insurance, so it's a whole new business. And Mr. Michael is careful: a pony can step on a foot. With a bunny, the worst case is a Bunny Scratch Survivor shirt. 🐰

🚙How is it so affordable?

That's our trick: we fit at least ten animals into a Ford Escape. A whole party in a small footprint means we can charge a fair rate.

⭐What's the Gold membership?

That gold trophy is your key. It unlocks Gold: a free farm visit for 2, a free $25 farm gift, the Gold web app, and about 40% off big stuff like mobile petting zoos, camps and field trips. An Android app is on the way.

💜Do you help schools or groups on a budget?

Yes! Our Violet ticket, named for our daughter, is a discounted rate for schools, churches and families in need.

📣Why don't you advertise?

"We barely pay for ads. We put that energy into giving instead. We try to give at least twice as much as we receive."

🎁What do you give away?

"Lots! Farm tours come with Gold, and we donate tours to churches and schools for raffles and silent auctions, often 3, 5, even 10 at a time. We give away plants we grow on the farm, like elephant ears, milkweed and lemongrass, plus fun thrift gifts like sports cards, comics and toys."

🌿Are the plants really free?

"Most are! We grew and divided them, so now we have plenty to share, usually potted in good soil. For a really big one, like a 6 to 10 foot elephant ear, we might ask a small donation."

🙌Can my kid hold one?

"I'll hold them so they feel safe. Come pet right here!" At public events, only helpers hold or pick up animals.

🥕Why can't we feed them?

"We keep their food just right so they stay healthy. They'd love some gentle pets though!"

🧼Are they safe and clean?

"They're our family animals, cared for every day on our farm. We're insured, which gives hosts peace of mind. Like any farm visit, wash up after petting."

🎈How do I book?

"Head to sheehanhomestead.com/services or call Michael at 914-263-1311. Want me to text him your name?"

🎂Do you do birthdays, schools, churches or senior centers?

"Yes to all of it! Birthdays, schools, churches, senior centers, festivals. Check sheehanhomestead.com/services."

📍Where's the farm? Can we visit?

"We're in Callahan, and yes! We have Visit Us days at the farm. Check sheehanhomestead.com/services to grab a spot."

Something tricky or a complaint? Smile and call Michael or Ann-Marie.

8 Gig types & setup

🎒 Leaving the farm

Unless it's a repeat customer, bring the 3D printed trophy and the prize wheel signs for free gifts. They tell people who we are, which brings more gigs, which means more work and more pay for you.

🐰 Bunny Haven

Bunnies only, usually 4 to 5 of them. Sweet and simple.

👵 Senior centers, PPEC and VPK

PPEC is medical daycare, and VPK is pre-K. These are usually weekday gigs and usually easier ones. They're businesses, but we price them close to a donation, and they often pay us 2 to 3 months late through corporate, so we usually pay you out of our own pocket. The margin is small, but we love serving seniors, medical daycares and little learners, and we want you working.

🎪 Bigger festivals

Weekdays and holidays are schools and preschools. Weekends are big communities, golf courses and churches. Fall and spring Saturdays are our busiest days. Expect 2 helpers unless it's a small one.

🏗️ Setup

🚧

At least two mobile panel fences, clipped together with hair ties. They're mobile so you can move if it rains or the sun shifts.

⛺

A vendor tent for shade, plus water and hand sanitizer.

🚪 Who comes in the pen?

🏡

Backyard parties: kids may come inside the pen. Say "big step!" as they step in.

🎡

Public and larger events: nobody comes in, and only helpers pick up animals.

10 What you can expect from us

💬

Ask before you accept. You can always ask what a gig pays before you say yes.

🙅

You never have to take a gig you don't want. If a neighborhood, a group (like special needs or very sick guests), a day or a time doesn't feel right, just say "no thank you." No explanation needed. We've done every one of these gigs ourselves, so we get it.

📅

Your calendar invite. Once you're on a gig, Ann-Marie sends a Google Calendar invite with the address and the host's cell number. Tap accept so we know you're in.

💵

Rates. About $20 an hour for easier gigs, $25 an hour for delivery or harder gigs.

⏱️

Pay timing. We usually pay same day or within 24 to 48 hours. Please allow up to 7 days. Payments go out by hand right now (Ann-Marie presses the button, and we have a 9-month-old), and a real pay system is on the way. Most businesses pay contractors in 15 to 30 days or by mailed check, so thanks for a little slack. Please don't ask to be paid before the work is done.

🌟

Bonuses, our way of saying thanks. We may add a bonus for a longer drive than expected, a very hot day, going above and beyond (like great video), lots of wheel sign-ups, or a sign-up that turns into a booking. Bonuses are up to us and never guaranteed.
